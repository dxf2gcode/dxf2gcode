# Getting started

Start with a small drawing before preparing your own job.

1. [Install and start](../installation.md): choose MSI, portable ZIP or a source checkout.
2. [First DXF to G-code](first-dxf-to-gcode.md): follow a complete rectangle example.
3. [Find your way around](../user-guide/interface-and-navigation.md): understand
   Geometry, Machining, selection, the canvas and messages.

Continue with [Drawing and project](../user-guide/drawing/index.md) to work with
your own files, or [Tools](../user-guide/tools/index.md) to prepare the tool table.
